# Raw notes: player (unverified, from reading the code of version 1.4.0, 2026-11-22)

Working notes of the reader who wrote part of the spec. Line numbers fit that version only.


Conventions: positions are the sprite's top-left, 8.8 fixed (PX(v) = integer pixel = v>>8). Player sprite 16x16, body 12 wide.
"8.8 value" N means N/256 px (or px/frame). FX(i,f) = i*256+f. 60 ticks per second. Lines are in src/game/.
"pressed" = button edge this tick (down now, not down last tick); "pad" = held. CPU partner gets a synthetic pad (cpu.c) and goes through exactly the same code.

====================================================================
PART A: RULES
====================================================================

--- A0. Tick order (game.c:807 update_play, player.c:488 player_update) ---
R1. Per play tick (game.c:807-838): Start-button handling; g_level_frames++; g_freeze-- (if >0); player 1 update, then player 2 update; door check; if nobody is PS_ALIVE the tick ends here (room stands still: no bubbles/enemies/items/effects, vacuums do not tick, level timers keep their values; but player_update of dying/rescue players still ran); then bubbles_update (which calls vacuums_tick, bubble.c:570), enemies_update, chaser, boss, items_update, effects_update, counters_check, powerup_round_tick, bonus_round_tick. Player 0 is always processed before player 1; items are processed after both players.
R2. PS_ALIVE player tick order (player.c:511-555): invulnerable-- (if >0); stun-- (if >0, and then pad=0, pressed=0 for the whole tick); if carried by a water stream: dash=0, touch check, done (nothing else); if on_ground: dash_used=0; dash_tick (cooldown/glow count down); trail record; then EITHER dash (if dash running or a dash starts now): dash_move + enemies_dash_hit, OR walk + jump_and_fall; then shoot (cooldowns, push bubble, vacuum, fire); touch_bubbles; Steam Whistle touch (only if g_freeze); enemy/shot/boss touch (only if not safe) -> hurt.
R3. Player states: PS_OFF (not in game), PS_ALIVE, PS_DYING, PS_ENTERING (drifting to the start point at round start), PS_RESCUE (in rescue bubble). player.c:131 internal.h.

--- A1. Start position, spawn ---
R4. Player 1 spawns at pixel x=24, player 2 at x=256-24-16=216, both at y=200 (floor: FIELD_BOTTOM 224 -8 -16). Player 1 faces right, player 2 faces left (facing_left = id). player.c:33-36, internal.h:53-54.
R5. player_start (player.c:38-59): ALIVE, on_ground=1, shoot cooldown/pose 0, invulnerable=120, vx=vy=0, stun/carried/rescue/dash state all cleared (dash buffer, cooldown, ready, used = 0). Used for: first join, respawn after death, and the freed-at-round-end case. Powers and letters are NOT touched by player_start.
R6. Joining: lives = 3 (game.c:10,617). Max lives 9 (game.c:21). Extra life at 30,000 and 100,000 points (game.c:125-128, check_extra_lives game.c:775), once each per player (extends_given), +1 life only if lives < 9 (the extend counts as given even when at 9 lives), plays SFX_EXTRA.

--- A2. Walking (player.c:327 walk) ---
R7. Normal: acceleration 0.207 px/f^2 (WALK_ACCEL 8.8=53), max 0.918 px/f (235). Player 1 and 2 identical. Shoes power-up: acceleration 0.3125 (80), max 1.289 (330=FX(1,74)). player.c:10-11,18-19.
R8. Holding Left: vx -= accel, clamp to -max, facing=left. Else holding Right: same mirrored, facing=right. Left beats Right when both held. With neither held: vx moves toward 0 by accel per tick, never overshoots (snaps to 0). Same acceleration in air and on ground (no air-control difference). player.c:333-349.
R9. Facing only changes by Left/Right pad input (not by dash, not by vx).
R10. Movement step: if vx != 0, check the wall ahead (level_wall_ahead: at feet row, the pixel in front of the body edge (edge = x+2 when going left, x+13 when going right); blocked only if the edge pixel is free and the next pixel in direction is solid). Blocked -> vx=0 (speed lost, no move). Else x += vx, then x clamped so the body centre (x+8) lies in 22..234 (sprite x 14..226). level.c:113-130, player.c:351-363.
R11. The feet-row-only wall test means a body is blocked by walls only at its bottom row (y+15); head/torso do not collide with side walls.

--- A3. Jump, gravity, landing (player.c:366 jump_and_fall) ---
R12. Jump button is B, level-triggered ("held", pad & PAD_B), not edge: if the player stands on the ground and B is held, the jump starts (also when B was already held while landing: holding B bunny-hops continuously). No jump buffering, no coyote time. player.c:368-378.
R13. Jump: vy = -2.566 px/f (JUMP_SPEED 8.8 = 657); the player moves by vy in the same tick; counts g_count.jumps (saturates at 255; rate-candy unlock at 35); SFX_JUMP. Gravity is not applied in the tick of take-off.
R14. In the air (or any tick not (on_ground and vy>=0)): if B is NOT held and vy < -0.848 (JUMP_CUT 217) then vy = -0.848 (cut). Then gravity: vy += 0.078 (GRAVITY_UP 20) while vy<0, else vy += 0.3125 (GRAVITY_DOWN 80) (decided by the sign of vy BEFORE adding); fall speed capped at 2.0 px/f (MAX_FALL). player.c:382-390.
R15. Resulting heights (computed from the numbers): full jump held = about 43 px (11121/256; code comment says 42), apex after 33 ticks; tapped jump about 6-7 px.
R16. Rising: y += vy, never blocked by platforms or ceilings in the room (one-way platforms). Only the top of the playfield stops it: if the new y is < FIELD_Y (16) or wrapped (>=224) then y=16 and vy=0. player.c:392-398.
R17. Falling: level_fall moves down one pixel at a time (not more than the fall distance) and stops on the first ground; on landing the sub-pixel is cleared (y &= 0xFF00), vy=0, on_ground=1. Falling past the bottom (y>=224) wraps to the top of the playfield (level.c:88-109). level.c:68-78 on_ground: feet pixel (y+15) free AND the pixel below solid, tested at x+4 and at x+11 (either one is enough). The top border row of the room only counts as ground at the side walls (tiles 0-1 and 30-31), so nothing stands on the ceiling (level.c:44-52).
R18. on_ground is recomputed at the start of jump_and_fall (so it is stale during a dash).

--- A4. Dash (Plus only) ---
R19. Mode gate: all dash code is inert unless g_opt.plus (player.c:90, 77). Buttons: L = dash left, R = dash right; D-pad ignored; facing does not change. A press of L+R in the same tick is ignored (player.c:95).
R20. Press -> dash_buffer = 6, buffered direction. try_dash runs every tick when no dash is running (player.c:537): if buffer != 0 it is decremented first, then if a dash is running/used/cooling it returns (the press is "kept"), else the dash starts. So a press is honoured on the press tick and up to 5 ticks later (6 checks in all; design guide says "6 frames").
R21. Dash start (player.c:106-113): dash = 12 ticks, direction = buffered direction (the LAST press wins if two presses are buffered), buffer 0, dash_used = 1, vy = 0 (jump momentum is dropped), last_free_x remembered, SFX_DASH.
R22. Dash movement (player.c:117): each tick x += or -= 4.5 px (DASH_SPEED 8.8 = 1152) -> 12 ticks = 54 px; vx=0; y unchanged and no gravity (the player hovers); outer walls clamp x (centre 22..234); inner walls, platforms are ignored. The start tick itself also moves 4.5 px (try_dash then dash_move in the same tick).
R23. While dashing: invulnerable (safe()), pops bubbles in a band: |dx|<14 and |dy|<20 (top-left difference in px) instead of 12x12; never bounces on bubbles; enemies_dash_hit each dash tick (a shield enemy within |dx|<14,|dy|<14 loses its plate: becomes a plain walker, dazed 60 ticks, SFX_TRAP, burst effect). Shooting and push bubble and vacuum still work during a dash (shoot() runs after).
R24. Dash end (player.c:125-130): on the 12th tick dash_cooldown = 60 and invulnerable = max(invulnerable, 30). So invulnerability covers the whole dash plus the last dash tick's checks plus 29 more ticks.
R25. Dash cooldown: dash_tick counts it down each tick BEFORE try_dash; the next dash is possible on the 60th tick after the one the dash ended on. When it reaches 0, dash_ready = 10 ticks of yellow glow (Plus draws an aura sprite). No glow after the very first dash of a life (cooldown was already 0).
R26. One dash per jump: dash_used is set at dash start and cleared at the start of any tick in which on_ground is true. In the air, on_ground is false (stale value from take-off) until landing. See H-decisions for the ground case.
R27. Dash ending inside a wall (player.c:131-147): "inside" = any of the four corners of the box (x+2..x+13, y+2..y+15) is in a solid tile. If so, the player is pushed on in the dash direction, 1 px at a time, up to 48 px, to the first position that is not inside; if the outer wall is reached first (position stops changing) or none found, the player returns to last_free_x (the last dash position that was not inside a wall, initially the start x). Check is done only on the final tick; mid-dash positions in walls are fine.
R28. Dash is cancelled (dash=0) by: rescue bubble (player.c:175), being carried by water (player.c:523), new round (player.c:262). Dash is not available while carried, stunned does not block a buffered dash (try_dash gets pressed=0 but the buffer keeps running and can fire).

--- A5. Shooting and aiming (player.c:408 aim, 420 shoot) ---
R29. Fire = Y, edge-triggered (pressed). Allowed when shoot_cooldown == 0. A press during cooldown is lost (no buffer). Cooldown 15 ticks (7 with rate power-up). shoot_cooldown and shoot_pose are decremented at the start of shoot(), before the checks, so with cooldown 15 the next shot is possible 15 ticks later. shoot_pose = 10 ticks (shoot graphic). SFX_SHOOT.
R30. Aim: Arcade/Classic: bubble spawns at x +-8 px in front of the player (facing), same y, flies left or right. Plus: if Up is held (pad) at the moment of the shot, the bubble spawns at (x, y-12) and flies up (AIR_UP), else as Classic. The same aim is used for the push bubble.
R31. A normal shot calls bubble_shoot(x,y,dir,powers): range power-up -> flies 14 ticks instead of 8; speed power-up -> 6 px/f instead of 4 (bubble.c:16,40-41,137-138). Each shot counts g_count.blown (cap 255; range-candy unlock at 35). In Plus a shot whose spawn point is inside a wall tile (centre test at +8,+8) bursts at once as a burst effect with SFX_POP, uses no bubble slot and does not count blown (bubble.c:121-128); Classic has no such test. If no free bubble slot (16 max), the shot is silently lost (but cooldown, pose, sound and ring bonus still happen).
R32. Fire Glove power-up: instead of a bubble a fireball effect starts, always sideways (aim-up is ignored), at x +-8 px, y = player y, direction = facing; no blown count (player.c:452-455).
R33. Bubble Ring power-up: every shot (bubble or fireball) adds +100 points to the shooter (score_add(score,2,1)), also when the bubble could not be created (player.c:459-461).
R34. Push bubble (Plus only): X pressed -> push_shoot(player, aim position, aim dir); only one push bubble per player may exist; if one is flying the press does nothing (no sound, no pose). On success shoot_pose=10 and SFX_SHOOT. No cooldown, free. Push bubble stats in bubble.c: speed 6 px/f, 12 ticks (about 72 px), hit box 10 (bubble.c:33-35). Order inside shoot(): X (push), then A (vacuum), then Y (fire), all can happen in one tick.

--- A6. Bubble contact, bounce (player.c:471 touch_bubbles) ---
R35. Every tick (not carried; also when stunned or invulnerable) the player is tested against all bubbles in state FLOAT or TRAPPED (not shot bubbles, not already popping). Contact = |dx|<12 and |dy|<12 between top-left pixels (dash: 14 / 20). overlap() in game.c:208.
R36. Contact pops the bubble (bubble_pop with the player's facing direction as chain direction, and the player as score owner) -> chain reaction (bubble.c). Exception, the bounce: if not dashing, vy > 0 (falling), B held, and the player's top is at least 8 px above the bubble's top (PX(py)+8 <= PX(by)) then no pop: vy = -2.566 (a full jump), SFX_JUMP. The bounce works on empty and trapped bubbles. No jump count, dash_used is not reset by a bounce.
R37. Empty bubble popped = +10 points for the owner of the chain (bubble.c:268-275 area, score_add(...,1,1)), counts g_count.popped (speed-candy unlock at 35). Trapped enemy popped = thrown enemy then food (see A9).

--- A7. Vacuum (Plus only; vacuum.c) ---
R38. A pressed (Plus only, only while PS_ALIVE, not stunned/carried): vacuum_start(player id, player x, y) + SFX_VACUUM. No cost, no cooldown, no limit. Each player has at most one vacuum (index = player id); pressing again restarts it at the new place (timer back to 90, grows anew). It stays where it was placed (does not follow the player). player.c:444-447, vacuum.c:149-156.
R39. Lifetime 90 ticks (VACUUM_FRAMES). Phases by age (= 90 - timer): age 0..7 growing (size scale 0,60,112,156,192,220,240,252 /256); age >= 8 and timer >= 12 full size with wobble; timer < 12 (last 11 active ticks, timer 11..1) shrinking (scale 254,249,240,228,212,192,169,142,112,78,41 for timer 11 down to 1... table index = timer: shrink_scale[timer]). vacuum.c:25-27,51,54,88-140.
R40. Shape: ellipse with half width rx and half height ry in px. Round radius 40 px. Scaled in growth/shrink: rx = ((640 - w)*s + 2048) >> 12, ry = ((640 + w)*s + 2048) >> 12 (w in 1/16 px, s = scale 0..256), min 1. At full size rx = (640 - w + 8)>>4, ry = (640 + w + 8)>>4.
R41. Wobble (full size only, from age 8): w from a 16-entry table advanced every 2 ticks: 0,24,45,59,64,59,45,24,0,-24,-45,-59,-64,-59,-45,-24 (1/16 px) -> period 32 ticks; w>0 = taller and narrower. Extremes: 36 wide x 44 high (w=64) and 44 x 36 (w=-64), +-4 px. Starts round (w=0) at age 8, tallest at age 16, round at 24, widest at 32.
R42. Span table: half width at distance dy from the middle line = (half_width[row] * rx + 20) / 40 with row = (dy*40 + ry/2)/ry and half_width[] the 41-entry quarter-circle table (40,39,39,...,8,0) (vacuum.c:44-47). Rebuilt only when rx or ry changes.
R43. Pulling (vacuum_move, vacuum.c:193), applied to: floating bubbles (empty, trapped, special, letter; not shot bubbles and not bubbles being shoved by a push bubble: bubble.c:595-600) and to a player in a rescue bubble (player.c:189). Nothing else (no enemies, items, players).
R44. An object is inside when |dy| <= ry and |dx| <= span[|dy|] where dx,dy are integer-pixel differences between the object's top-left and the player's top-left at placement (so effectively centre to centre). It is pulled even through walls (walls only matter with the developer switch DBG_VACUUM_WALLS).
R45. Pull speed depends on the tile distance "ring" = ring_of[|dy|>>3][|dx|>>3] (table vacuum.c:63-70): ring 0..5 -> speed 3.0, 2.5, 2.0, 1.5, 1.25, 1.0 px/f. If two vacuums cover the object the one with the smaller ring wins (tie: player 1's vacuum).
R46. Movement: one axis per tick, the axis with the bigger distance |dx| vs |dy| (tie: x axis) moves by the speed but never past the centre (exact 8.8 snap). At exactly the centre it is held (returns "handled", the room's air is skipped). Bubble is then clamped into the playfield (clamp_bubble).
R47. Vacuum shown as a coloured window around it on layer 0 + backdrop only (not sprites/HUD), lines clipped to the playfield (y 16..223), edges clipped to 0..255. Colour added: amount = 3 + pulse[(g_frame>>2)&7] with pulse 0,1,2,3,3,2,1,0 (3..6); red = amount>>1, green = amount - amount>>2, blue = amount; during the shrink amount *= scale/256. Window i belongs to player i. vacuum.c:253-316.
R48. Vacuum timers only run when bubbles_update runs (R1), i.e. not while everybody is dying/rescued; vacuums_clear at round start (bubble.c:67).

--- A8. Lives, death, rescue, respawn, invulnerability ---
R49. Contact check at the end of the tick (not when safe): enemies_touch (enemy.c:216): boss body -> DEADLY; any enemy shot ("rock", IT_ROCK, 8x8 overlap) -> DEADLY; Hurry-up chaser (10x10) -> DEADLY; any active, non-entering enemy within |dx|<10,|dy|<12 -> ENEMY (skipped entirely while the Steam Whistle freeze runs, but shots/chaser/boss still kill). First hit in that order wins.
R50. safe() = invulnerable>0 OR dash running OR debug switch (player.c:242).
R51. hurt (player.c:227): ENEMY touch with the co-op rescue option on AND the partner's state != PS_OFF -> rescue bubble. Any other case (DEADLY touch, rescue off, partner not in the game) -> PS_DYING, timer 0, carried 0, SFX_DEATH.
R52. Death animation: 120 ticks (DEATH_FRAMES); sprite frame (timer>>3)&3 of the 4 death cells; the dying player does not move and is not hurt again. At the tick the timer reaches 120 player_killed (game.c:720): lives -= 1 (if >0), ALL power-ups cleared (powers = 0), no-death flag cleared (no secret doors), enemies calmed down (not angry), Hurry-up chaser removed, all enemy shots removed, round timer g_level_frames = 0, hurry off. If lives remain: player_start (respawn at the start point, invulnerable 120, same room, the enemies stay as they are). Else PS_OFF; if nobody is left in the game: CONTINUE? countdown from 9 (one per second), Start rejoins with 3 lives and keeps the score; at 0 GAME OVER. Letters (BLASEN) and score are kept on death.
R53. Respawn/new-start invulnerability: 120 ticks (INVULN_FRAMES), counted down at the start of each alive tick (so 119 tick-ends are protected: when the counter has just reached 0 the tick is unprotected). Blink: sprite not drawn while (invulnerable & 4) -> 4 ticks on, 4 off. Dash grace uses the same counter/blink (30).
R54. Rescue bubble (co-op option, both modes): player becomes PS_RESCUE: vx=vy=0, dash=0, 420 ticks (7 s), drifts up first, SFX_UHHELPME. In the bubble the player is not hurt by anything (no contact check), cannot act, is not "alive" (items cannot be collected, bubbles do not pop). player.c:168-224.
R55. Drift: every 16 ticks (when rescue & 15 == 0, first at rescue = 416) the direction is read from the room's air at the player's centre (x+8,y+8); if the air is "none", a random direction 1..4 (up, down, left, right) is drawn (rng_next()&3 plus 1). Speed 0.35 px/f (RESCUE_SPEED 90). A vacuum overrides the drift. x clamped to the walls; y clamped to 22..200 (a y above 22 or wrapped is set to 22; below 200 is set to 200).
R56. Freed when the partner is PS_ALIVE and within |dx|<12,|dy|<12 of the bubble: state ALIVE, invulnerable 120, a burst item at the spot, SFX_POP, no life lost. Nothing else is reset (powers, letters kept; position kept; dash flags stay as set when trapped: dash=0 only).
R57. At rescue == 140 (2/3 of the time gone) SFX_UHHELPME again. When it runs out (420 ticks): PS_DYING (timer 0, SFX_DEATH) = the normal death, a life is lost. Sprite: ring colour red when rescue < 90 (and then blinks, hidden when rescue&4), orange when < 210, else normal; the player graphic is the falling pose.
R58. New round (player_enter_round, player.c:248): OFF stays OFF; a dying player loses the life now (player_killed, possibly dropping out); a rescued player is restarted (player_start); then everybody still in: dash/buffer/cooldown/ready/stun/carried cleared, state ENTERING, vx=vy=0, shoot pose 0. ENTERING: each tick x and y both move 1 px toward the start point (diagonal, moves on both axes at once); drawn as a bubble sprite (frame (g_frame>>3)&1); on arrival ALIVE, facing = id, on_ground=1. Not hurt while entering. The Fire Glove is removed at every round start (game.c:524); other power-ups are kept.

--- A9. Items (item.c) ---
R59. Item table: 16 slots (MAX_ITEMS). Spawning takes the first free slot; if none is free the item is silently dropped. Kinds: THROWN (defeated enemy flying), FOOD, BURST (pop effect), POPUP (score text), ROCK (enemy shots, soot), POWERUP, BONUS.
R60. Thrown enemy (item_spawn_thrown, item.c:68): g_count.defeated++ (cap 255, always, even when no slot; Steam Whistle unlock at 40). Start velocity: horizontal 1.5 + (rng&127)/256 px/f (1.5..2.0) in the given direction, vertical -(3.0 + (rng&127)/256) (up). Gravity 0.094 (24)/tick, max fall 2.0. It can pass through all platforms and walls horizontally; at the side limits (centre <22 or >234) vx reverses and it is placed at x 14 / 226. It bounces off the ceiling (y min 24, vy=0). For the first 40 ticks it cannot land and wraps through the bottom to the top; after 40 ticks it lands on the first ground it falls onto (level_fall, same ground test as the player) and turns into FOOD with the same tier, lifetime 600. item.c:223-257. The sprite cycles 4 frames every 4 ticks.
R61. Food: tier t is worth 1000 << t: 1000, 2000, 4000, 8000, 16000, 32000, 64000 (tiers 0..6). Chain kills give tier = min(kills so far in the chain, 6) (bubble.c:247); items turn enemies into tier 3 (8000 "gems", GEM_TIER); Steam Whistle touch gives tier 2 (4000). Collected by touching (|dx|<12,|dy|<12) by a PS_ALIVE player, player 1 checked first. +score, g_count.food++ (cap 255, Soap Star unlock at 10), SFX_GEM, becomes a popup showing the value. item.c:259-270,315-326.
R62. Food lives 600 ticks (10 s), then disappears; visible always while timer >= 120, then blinks 4 on/4 off (draw only if !(timer&4)). Food does not fall or move once landed.
R63. Popup: rises 0.39 px/f (POPUP_SPEED 100) for 60 ticks (about 23 px) or until y < 4; drawn 32 px wide centered over the item (x-8, clamped 0..224). Burst: 16 ticks, picture A for the first 8, picture B for the last 8; a rock hitting a wall bursts for 8 ticks (B only).
R64. Round bonus food (item.c:166): exactly when the round's frame counter equals 600 (10 s) one bonus item appears at the spawn point of the level's LAST listed enemy (x = slot.x*8, y = 16 + slot.y*8 - 8); if that enemy is a bouncer the first enemy's slot is used. Not placed if the level has no enemies. Type = (0-based level index) & 3: 0 cherry 500, 1 apple 700, 2 melon 1000, 3 cake 2000. Lives 600 ticks, blinks in the last 120. Once per round (reset by items_clear). Touch gives the value directly, SFX_GEM, popup 500/700/1000/2000.
R65. Power-up item (item.c:105): created at round frame 300 (effect.c:35,503) at the first listed enemy's spawn point, at most one per round, taking the lowest-numbered queued power (order RANGE, SPEED, RATE, SHOES, UMBRELLA, STAR, WAVE, WHISTLE, GLOVE, RING). Lifetime 600, blinking in the last 120. Collected like food (12x12 overlap, PS_ALIVE, player 1 first), SFX_EXTRA, becomes a popup.
R66. Unlock thresholds (effect.c:38-47; counters shared by both players; reset to 0 when reached; queued as bits): 35 bubbles blown -> RANGE; 35 empty bubbles popped -> SPEED; 35 jumps -> RATE; 3360 walking ticks (a tick in which the integer x changes; comment "15 times across the room") -> SHOES; 5 water bubbles popped -> UMBRELLA; 10 food collected -> STAR; 6 enemies washed away -> WAVE; 40 enemies defeated -> WHISTLE; 4 enemies burnt -> GLOVE; 3 candies (RANGE, SPEED or RATE collected; not SHOES) -> RING.
R67. Power-up effects (item.c:121 collect_powerup):
  - RANGE / SPEED / RATE / SHOES / GLOVE / RING (the default branch): the bit is set in the collector's powers; +100 points; popup "100". RANGE, SPEED, RATE also count one candy toward the Ring (cap 255). Effects: see R7, R31, R29, R32, R33. Kept until that player loses a life (death clears all), except GLOVE which is also removed at each round start.
  - UMBRELLA: +200, popup 200, asks to skip 3 rounds (round_skip(3)); takes effect only in normal rooms (game.c:841, other room kinds ignore it); the message names the collector.
  - STAR: every active (non-entering) enemy becomes a thrown 8000 gem, every trapped bubble too; the count n runs through multikill_award for the collector (3rd kill = multikill sound and a queued letter...); +1000, popup 1000.
  - WAVE: three water streams start at x=40, 120, 200 at y=24 with directions right, left, right (k&1 ? left : right), then the same as STAR; +1000.
  - WHISTLE: g_freeze = 480 ticks (8 s) (replaces a running freeze with 480), +1000, popup 1000. During the freeze enemies do not move/harm; a player touching one (12x12) defeats it: thrown tier-2 (4000) food; the group touched in one tick counts as a multikill. g_freeze is cleared at a new round and counted down every play tick.
R68. Enemy shots (IT_ROCK) in item.c: rock 2.0 px/f, spit 2.5, down shot 2.5 (internal.h:188-190). Rock and spit fly sideways until the centre is <22 or >234 px or a solid tile at (cx, y+8); then they burst (8 ticks). The down shot falls straight and is removed when y >= 200 (passes platforms, no burst). Soot (boss) moves by its own vx,vy, passes platforms, removed when centre x <16 or >240, y >= 208 or y < 16. They are not stopped or slowed by the freeze and are removed (rocks only) when a player dies.
R69. Score table (as in code): bubble popped +10; bonus food 500/700/1000/2000; food 1000..64000; Bubble Ring +100 per shot; power-up RANGE/SPEED/RATE/SHOES/GLOVE/RING +100; UMBRELLA +200; STAR/WAVE/WHISTLE +1000. Score has 8 digits (max 99,999,999, saturating at all 9s) text.c:132-150. Extra lives at 30,000 / 100,000.
R70. Draw order/details: player sprite pose: shoot pose first, else air (jump if vy<0, fall otherwise), else walk (2 frames every 8 ticks) if vx != 0, else stand (frame 0). Stun tints orange (stun is set by fire effects, 60 ticks). Dash tints yellow on alternate ticks and leaves two afterimages (Plus only). player.c:562-632.

====================================================================
PART B: HIDDEN DECISIONS
====================================================================

H1. Order inside a player tick: movement, then shooting, then bubble touch, then Whistle touch, then enemy touch. So a player that dashes into a bubble pops it the same tick and an enemy that touched in the same tick still kills at the end (unless safe). player.c:537-554.
H2. Jump is level-triggered: holding B makes the player re-jump the moment they land (bunny hop); there is no "press" requirement. player.c:368,372.
H3. Jump cut: only the cap is applied (-0.848) and only from the tick after take-off; tapping B shorter still gives a hop of about 6-7 px because take-off applies the full speed once. player.c:382-385.
H4. Gravity choice uses the sign of vy before the addition: at vy=0 the 0.3125 gravity applies. player.c:386.
H5. Walking is blocked by walls only at feet height (y+15), body above can pass walls; walls also zero vx completely (no sliding). level.c:113-121.
H6. Left/Right both held: left wins; Left/Right don't clear the opposite momentum, only decelerate by the acceleration. player.c:333.
H7. Aiming up is read from the HELD Up at the shot tick (not edge); Up also opens the exit door (pressed edge) in game.c:701, so shooting with Up near the door does both. player.c:410.
H8. Aiming glove: aim-up is ignored for the glove but the y offset logic for aim is not used (y = player y). player.c:454-455.
H9. Cooldown is decremented before the fire test, so the first shot after a shot comes exactly 15 ticks later; a Y press at tick 14 is lost (no buffer). player.c:425-427,450.
H10. The Bubble Ring bonus is paid even when the shot produced no bubble (no free slot, or bubble shot into a wall in Plus). player.c:459.
H11. Plus-only point-blank wall check: a bubble whose spawn point lies in a wall pops at once (Classic lacks the check, bubbles there simply start and pass through inner walls). It then also does not count toward the range unlock. bubble.c:114-128.
H12. Dash buffer: the press is honoured on the press tick and up to 5 ticks later (6 checks), although the design guide says 6 frames. The buffer is NOT cleared on landing, death, etc., only on use or timeout; buffering runs in Plus even while stunned (pressed=0 but a pre-stun buffer is still consumed). player.c:99-105.
H13. Two L/R presses within the buffer: the later one overwrites the direction. L+R together: ignored. player.c:95-98.
H14. dash_used is reset at the start of any tick with on_ground true, but on_ground is NOT updated during a dash. A ground dash therefore clears dash_used on its second tick (on_ground still 1); only the 60-tick cooldown limits ground dashes. An air dash keeps on_ground=0 so only landing resets. After an air dash, on_ground (stale 0) is recomputed in the first normal tick. player.c:530-532,370.
H15. The dash sets vy=0: a dash at the top of a jump or while rising kills the vertical speed; after the dash the player starts falling from vy=0, with the fall gravity.
H16. Dash invulnerability needs a separate path: the dash end sets invulnerable=30 in the same tick the dash counter hits 0 so there is no unprotected tick. If the player already had more than 30 invulnerability ticks it is not reduced. player.c:125-130.
H17. Dash cooldown starts when the dash ends (set at dash_move), and counts down in dash_tick before try_dash: first possible dash tick = 60th tick after the end tick. The "ready glow" only appears after cooldown reaches 0, so never after a dash-free life start. player.c:152-160.
H18. inside_wall tests only the four corners of a 12x14 box (x+2..x+13, y+2..y+15); a wall of 8 px width can lie between the corners (corners are 11 px apart) so the dash can end overlapping a one-tile wall without being detected. The extension search then does not run. player.c:81-86. (Looks like an accident for single-tile walls.)
H19. Dash extension distance is 48 px but each step is 1 px; stops when level_clamp_x refuses to move (outer wall) and then returns to last_free_x, which can be BEHIND the start of the wall run. player.c:131-147.
H20. The dash start tick already moves the player (try_dash and dash_move in the same tick) so the sound plays on the tick the first 4.5 px are done. player.c:537-538.
H21. During the dash the bubble band is 14x20 but the enemy dash-hit band is 14x14, and enemy bodies use 10x12. player.c:473,enemies.c:197.
H22. Bubble bounce rule: any falling player with B held lands on a bubble if their top is >= 8 px above the bubble top; B not held (even for a moment) pops it instead. A second overlapping bubble in the same tick is popped, because vy is no longer positive after the first bounce. Bounce does not reset dash_used or add to the jump counter. player.c:479-485.
H23. The bounce can be done on trapped enemy bubbles, so a bounce also keeps the enemy trapped; standing on a bubble pops it as soon as B is released. player.c:476.
H24. Bubble pop from touch uses the player's facing as chain direction (also during a dash, which does not turn the player), which decides which way a trapped enemy flies out at the first kill. player.c:483.
H25. Invulnerability counts down before the touch check, so a counter of 120 protects 119 ticks, not 120; the blink shows at (counter & 4). The counter keeps running during the dash and rescue does not run it. player.c:512-514,591.
H26. Rescue condition checks only that the partner is not PS_OFF: a partner who is dying, entering or himself in a rescue bubble counts. Two trapped players cannot free each other (the freeing partner must be PS_ALIVE) and both die when time runs out. player.c:229,205.
H27. A rescue bubble ignores deadly touch (shots, boss, chaser) entirely; the design guide says shots still kill, but in the code no contact check runs in PS_RESCUE. Looks like a divergence from the guide. player.c:179-224.
H28. Rescue drift: the first 16-tick direction read happens at rescue==416, so the first 4 ticks go up (initial dir). A random direction (RNG draw) is used only in cells with no airflow. rng_next draws: player.c:186 only.
H29. Rescue freed player: invulnerable 120, SFX_POP and a burst; powers and letters are kept; no life lost. Freed on contact even if the partner is invulnerable or dashing. player.c:205-211.
H30. Rescue running out calls death through PS_DYING (120-tick animation), and the debug counter 11. The player was NOT invulnerable at that moment; player_killed afterwards resets round timer and powers like any death. player.c:216-223.
H31. player_killed does clear "powers" including the glove, calms enemies, removes shots and resets round_frames to 0 (and thereby re-arms the 300/600-tick power-up and bonus placement checks, which use equality: they can fire again after a death if not yet "placed"). game.c:720-736, effect.c:507, item.c:170.
H32. Letters, score, extends_given stay after death; lives clamp at 9 only on the extend (extend consumed even if lives==9). game.c:777-781.
H33. player_enter_round for a dying player calls player_killed, which already calls player_start (position = start point) so the "drift to the start point" of that player is zero length; same for a rescued one (player_start). For a living player ENTERING does NOT set invulnerability (whatever was left stays). player.c:253-259,268.
H34. ENTERING moves x and y by exactly 1 px per tick each and snaps the sub-pixel to 0 (TO_POS); a player drifting is not blocked by walls. player.c:315-325,492-494.
H35. Hurt precedence: TOUCH_DEADLY (boss, shots, chaser) is returned before TOUCH_ENEMY by the order boss, items(rocks), chasers, enemies; only ENEMY contact can turn into a rescue bubble. enemy.c:216-243.
H36. The Whistle touch (multikill_award(enemies_defeat_at)) runs BEFORE the enemy touch each tick, so with a frozen enemy the player defeats it instead of dying; enemies_touch also ignores enemies during the freeze, but not shots. player.c:546-553, enemy.c:235.
H37. Item pickup checks only PS_ALIVE players and player 1 first; if both overlap in the same tick player 1 gets it. Whoever collects the power-up gets it. Counters are shared, so player 2 blowing bubbles can unlock a power-up that player 1 picks up. item.c:286-326.
H38. candies counter increments for RANGE, SPEED and RATE only (tier <= POWER_RATE), not SHOES; Ring unlocks after three of those collections. item.c:152.
H39. Power-up queue is a bit mask: the same power cannot be queued twice; the lowest index is placed first; only one power-up per round, at round frame 300 on the first enemy slot; if nothing is queued at frame 300 nothing appears that round (except after a death reset). effect.c:487-515.
H40. UMBRELLA still pays +200 and is consumed in non-normal rooms (boss, secret) but the skip is ignored. game.c:841-845.
H41. Wave creates the three water streams before turning enemies to gems; both Star and Wave call enemies_to_gems then bubbles_trapped_to_gems then multikill_award(n), n = enemies + trapped; entering enemies are not affected. Gems alternate throw direction by slot index (enemy i odd -> left; bubble i odd -> left). item.c:132-145, enemy.c:152-164, bubble.c:196-209.
H42. Thrown RNG: two draws per thrown enemy (first horizontal then vertical) and none when no item slot is free (still counted as defeated). Chain kills also draw one RNG number per additional kill for direction (bubble.c:248). rng use in vacuum.c: none. rng in item.c: lines 75,77 only. item.c:68-79.
H43. Thrown: for the first 40 ticks no collision with floors at all, and the y wraps at the bottom (level_wrap_y) back to the top; after 40 ticks it lands on the first ground (so it can land far from where it died). The horizontal bounce uses only room edges, never inner walls. item.c:223-257.
H44. Thrown vy < 0: while rising it cannot land (rising branch first), so landing is only checked when vy >= 0 and timer == 0. When it hits the ceiling y=24 and vy=0 (then gravity pulls it down). item.c:238-243.
H45. The thrown x reset on a side bounce sets x to 14 or 226 (both pixels) discarding the sub-pixel and the direction test uses the NEW vx sign. item.c:229-231.
H46. Bonus food equality test (round frame == 600): if the round counter is reset by a death, the bonus can only appear once (bonus_placed), after the counter reaches 600 again. If placement fails (no slot) it is lost for the round. item.c:170-173.
H47. Bonus position: last enemy slot of the room, but if that enemy is a bouncer (can start mid-air) slot 0 is used, even if slot 0 is a bouncer too. Spawn y = row*8 + 16 - 8 (8 px above the enemy's row, i.e. standing on the tile). item.c:174-178.
H48. Bonus type uses the 0-based level index & 3, not the displayed round number; secret/boss levels use their own index. item.c:179.
H49. Food timer 600 includes the blinking; blinking on timer&4 so 4 visible/4 hidden ticks; the item is still collectable while "hidden". item.c:356-358.
H50. Popup uses the slot of the collected item (kind changed in place); it rises and ends at 60 ticks or y<4. A popup does not block anything but occupies one of 16 slots. item.c:328-333.
H51. Enemy shots touch the player with an 8x8 overlap but are drawn at 16x16. Rocks burst on a solid centre pixel at (x+8, y+8) only; they are stopped by thin features between ticks? speed 2-2.5 px < tile so no tunnelling. item.c:216-219.
H52. The Soot ball ignores platforms; the Down shot ignores platforms and ends at y>=200 without a burst. item.c:198-212.
H53. vacuum_move uses the vacuum's span for THIS frame's shape (shape() runs in vacuums_tick after the decrement, and in vacuum_start). So the pulling area equals the area drawn. A vacuum restarted shows rx=ry=1 for that frame. vacuum.c:149-170.
H54. In vacuum_start the span is computed from the old rx/ry cache if equal; the first ever vacuum starts with rx=ry=0 so a table is built. Harmless.
H55. A vacuum's `timer` reaching 0 happens inside vacuums_tick, so the last pulling tick is timer==1 (shrunk to scale 41: about 6x6 px). vacuum.c:163-169,54.
H56. shrink_scale[0] is never used (timer 0 = off). The comment "scale ... frames left" has 12 entries but timer=11 is the first. vacuum.c:54,96.
H57. vacuum_move picks the nearest vacuum by ring, not by pixel distance, and ties go to player 1 (strict <). A bubble inside two vacuums goes to the nearer ring. vacuum.c:209.
H58. Pull step: axis with bigger |difference| first, tie -> x; uses true 8.8 differences though the inside test used integer pixels, so objects stop exactly on the centre (held forever until the vacuum ends). If both differences are 0 it returns "held" and the bubble does not follow the air. vacuum.c:219-246.
H59. Vacuums pull bubbles through walls; only the debug switch DBG_VACUUM_WALLS (not shipped) blocks them. vacuum.c:188-191.
H60. Shot (flying) bubbles and bubbles shoved by a push bubble are not pulled (the shove branch comes first in bubble.c:595-598); the pull applies to floating, trapped, special and letter bubbles alike. A trapped enemy inside is dragged with the bubble.
H61. The vacuum is placed with the player's top-left, but "inside" uses top-left of the object too, so the pull circle is centred on the player's centre for 16x16 objects. If the player dies the vacuum stays until its timer ends (timers run as long as bubbles_update runs). vacuum.c:203-206.
H62. Vacuum window math avoids divisions: the quotient tables are stepped; results identical to the written formulas (comment). Edge rounding: the "+ RADIUS/2" and "+ ry/2" terms round to nearest. vacuum.c:77-140.
H63. The window colour of vacuum i uses window i; both windows use the same fixed colour on real hardware, "the stronger of their colours" (game.h comment) so two vacuums look the same colour as the stronger one. vacuum.c:253-266, game.h:92.
H64. Classic vs Plus differences found here: (a) no dash (L/R do nothing), (b) no push bubble (X), (c) no vacuum (A), (d) no aim-up (Up held does nothing for shooting), (e) no point-blank wall pop (bubble.c) and fired bubbles may pass inner walls (design guide, bubble.c), (f) no dash afterimages/glow, (g) rescue bubble and extra lives behave the same in both. Everything else in player.c/item.c is mode-independent.

H66. The Whistle pick-up replaces (not adds to) the running freeze value; picking a second one resets to 480. item.c:147.
H67. Dash pop uses bubble_pop with no special chain id: "one dash = one chain" is done in bubble.c:627-636 by keeping chain_kills while player_dash_invulnerable(owner) is true, so a chain popped by a dash ends only after the dash ended and nothing pops. (Interplay between files; see bubble.c.)
H68. Walking counter (shoes unlock) counts ticks with a changed integer x (not pixels), so shoes need 3360 such ticks; with moving > 1 px/f (shoes) a tick still counts as 1. player.c:359-361.
H69. Stun (fire): pad and pressed are zeroed for the whole player tick, so a stunned player decelerates, cannot jump (falls), cannot shoot, but cooldowns still run, the dash buffer still runs, bubble contacts and enemy contacts still happen, and the invulnerability counter and the stun counter run. player.c:515-519.
H70. Carried by water: only the contact check runs; the dash is cancelled but dash_cooldown is not set; bubble touch is skipped (carried returns before it). player.c:520-528.
H71. The player's body used for enemy touch is 10x12 (enemy side), for item pickup 12x12, for bubble contact 12x12 (14x20 dash); the sprite is 16x16 and walls use 12 px wide (x+2..x+13). Different boxes are used for different things on purpose-less: no single hit box. player.c, enemy.c:237, item.c:288.
H72. check_extra_lives is called every tick of play (and in the boss branch); the extend is awarded one tick after the score crosses the limit and can be awarded for both thresholds on different ticks if the score jumps past both; one per tick. game.c:775-784,870,905.
